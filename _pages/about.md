I am **Harshith Gowrachari**, a **Postdoctoral Researcher** at **ONERA - The French Aerospace Lab**, Toulouse, France.

I currently work on the EU-funded [**Impact Monitor 2**](https://impactmonitor.eu/im2/) project with DLR and NLR, contributing to the **environmental impact assessment of aviation**. I perform **Large Eddy Simulations (LES)** of aircraft pollutant dispersion with **online chemistry** at the **local airport level**, using the **Meso-NH** atmospheric model. Further, I develop **reduced order** and **multi-fidelity models** that enable fast evaluation of the many scenarios required for impact assessment.

I am the developer and maintainer of **miiPy**, an open-source Python library for data-driven system identification, interpolatory model reduction, and operator inference.

## Research Interests

- **Computational fluid dynamics:** reactive and multiphase flows, heat transfer, aerodynamics, and aeroacoustics
- **Reduced order modelling:** projection-based, data-driven, and nonlinear methods, especially for **advection-dominated problems**
- **Numerical methods:** finite volume, finite element, and finite difference methods for PDEs
- **Applications:** aerospace, metallurgical processes, energy, and, more recently, atmospheric physics
